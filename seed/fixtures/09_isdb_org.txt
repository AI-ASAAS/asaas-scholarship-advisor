Title: Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) Scholarship Programme
Official URL: https://www.isdb.org/scholarships
Overview: Premier development-focused scholarship programme supporting outstanding students from IsDB member countries.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Citizens of IsDB member countries including Yemen.
- Academic merit with minimum very good (B+) grades in previous degree.
- Commitment to return and contribute to national socio-economic development.
Selection Criteria:
- Development impact of study
- Academic excellence
- Leadership potential
Fields of Study: Sustainable Agriculture, Renewable Energy, Water Resources Management, Health Sciences, Data Science
Funding: Full university tuition, monthly living allowance commensurate with cost of living, medical coverage, and round-trip economy airfare.
Required Documents: Certified graduation certificates, Official transcripts, English/French language proficiency proof, Work experience letters
Deadline: February (2027-02-28)
How to Apply: Submit online application through the IsDB scholarship portal.
