Title: Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Programme
Official URL: https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/
Overview: Hungarian state scholarship programme offering full degree studies at leading Hungarian universities.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Citizens of eligible sending partners including Yemen.
- Applicants must meet university admission requirements and hold prerequisite diplomas.
Selection Criteria:
- Academic excellence
- Institutional entrance exam and interview
Fields of Study: Engineering, Computer Science, Agricultural Sciences, Natural Sciences, Economics, Medicine
Funding: Full tuition fee exemption, monthly stipend, free dormitory accommodation or HUF 40,000/month housing allowance, and medical insurance.
Required Documents: Online application form, Motivation letter, Proof of language proficiency, School certificates and transcripts, Medical certificate
Deadline: 15 January (2027-01-15)
How to Apply: Submit via the DreamApply portal and obtain nomination from the partner sending authority.
