Title: Chevening Scholarships
Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/
Overview: The UK government's global scholarship programme offering full financial support for one-year master's degrees at any UK university.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Be a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country including Yemen.
- Return to your country of citizenship for a minimum of two years after your award has ended.
- Have completed an undergraduate degree that will enable you to gain entry into a postgraduate programme at a UK university.
- Have at least two years (equivalent to 2,800 hours) of work experience.
Selection Criteria:
- Demonstrated leadership and influence qualities
- Clear career plan and commitment to home country development
Fields of Study: Public Policy, International Relations, Economics, Renewable Energy, Public Health, Law
Funding: Full university tuition fees, monthly living allowance, return economy flight to the UK, and essential arrival and departure grants.
Required Documents: Undergraduate degree certificate, Academic transcripts, Two professional/academic reference letters, Three UK university course choices
Deadline: November (2027-11-03)
How to Apply: Apply via the online application system on the official Chevening website.
