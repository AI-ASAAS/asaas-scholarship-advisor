Title: DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme (Public Policy & Good Governance)
Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/
Overview: Prestigious DAAD programme supporting future leaders from developing countries in governance and public policy fields.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Graduates from developing countries including Yemen.
- First higher education degree obtained with above-average results.
- Degree completed within the last six years.
Selection Criteria:
- Academic merit
- Professional motivation related to good governance and development
Fields of Study: Public Policy, Good Governance, International Relations, Public Management, Peace and Conflict Studies
Funding: Monthly scholarship payment of €934, health insurance contribution, travel subsidy, and 6-month preparatory German language course.
Required Documents: DAAD application form, Hand-signed motivation letter, Curriculum Vitae (Europass), Copies of academic degrees and transcripts
Deadline: 31 July (2027-07-31)
How to Apply: Submit applications directly to the participating German master's programmes of choice.
