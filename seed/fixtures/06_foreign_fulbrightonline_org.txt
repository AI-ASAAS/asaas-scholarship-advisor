Title: Fulbright Foreign Student Program
Official URL: https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/
Overview: Flagship international educational exchange program sponsored by the U.S. government for master's studies in the USA.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Yemeni citizens residing in Yemen or eligible international locations.
- Hold a bachelor's degree with strong academic record.
- Fluency in English (TOEFL or equivalent).
Selection Criteria:
- Academic and leadership potential
- Civic engagement
- Cross-cultural communication skills
Fields of Study: All academic fields excluding clinical medical studies
Funding: Full tuition fees, monthly living stipend, health benefit plan, and international travel round-trip.
Required Documents: Official academic transcripts, Three letters of recommendation, Personal statement, Study objectives essay
Deadline: 1 June (2027-06-01)
How to Apply: Applications are submitted through the Fulbright online portal managed by the U.S. Embassy / AMIDEAST.
