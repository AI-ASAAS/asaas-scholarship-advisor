Title: Eiffel Excellence Scholarship Program (Campus France)
Official URL: https://www.campusfrance.org/en/the-eiffel-scholarship-program-of-excellence
Overview: French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs scholarship designed to enable French higher education institutions to attract top foreign students.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Non-French foreign citizens from developing countries including Yemen.
- Up to 27 years of age for master's level, up to 32 for PhD level.
Selection Criteria:
- Academic excellence
- Institution's international policy
- Candidate's career project
Fields of Study: Engineering and Exact Sciences, Economics and Management, Law and Political Science
Funding: Monthly allowance of €1,181 for master's (€1,800 for PhD), international round-trip airfare, national health insurance, and cultural activities.
Required Documents: Curriculum Vitae in French or English, Professional project essay, Academic transcripts, Language proficiency certificates
Deadline: 10 January (2027-01-10)
How to Apply: Applications must be submitted by French higher education institutions on behalf of admitted candidates.
