Title: Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC - Silk Road & Bilateral)
Official URL: https://www.campuschina.org/
Overview: Full scholarships established by the Ministry of Education of China to sponsor international students.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Non-Chinese citizens in good health.
- Citizens of Belt and Road countries including Yemen.
- Bachelor applicants under 25, master's under 35, doctoral under 40.
Selection Criteria:
- Academic merit
- Research proposal quality
- Supervisor acceptance (pre-admission)
Fields of Study: Civil Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, International Trade, Medicine, Materials Science
Funding: Tuition waiver, free on-campus university accommodation, comprehensive medical insurance, and monthly living stipend (CNY 2,500 - 3,500).
Required Documents: Application form for Chinese Government Scholarship, Notarized highest diploma, Academic transcripts, Study plan in China
Deadline: 30 March (2027-03-30)
How to Apply: Apply via the Campus China online portal (CSC System) and directly to chosen Chinese universities.
