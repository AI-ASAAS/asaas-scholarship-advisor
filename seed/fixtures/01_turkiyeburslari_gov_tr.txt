Title: Türkiye Bursları (Turkish Government Scholarship)
Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/
Overview: Comprehensive full government scholarship for undergraduate, master's, and doctoral studies in Turkish universities.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Citizens of all countries including Yemen are eligible to apply.
- Minimum academic achievement of 70% for bachelor's, 75% for master's and PhD, and 90% for health sciences.
- Under 21 years of age for undergraduate, under 30 for master's, under 35 for PhD.
Selection Criteria:
- Academic excellence
- Social and extracurricular engagement
- Interview performance
Fields of Study: Engineering, Medicine, Social Sciences, Humanities, Natural Sciences, Information Technology
Funding: Full university tuition, monthly stipend, free student dormitory accommodation, one-off return flight ticket, and health insurance.
Required Documents: National ID or valid passport, Diploma or temporary graduation certificate, Academic transcripts, Statement of purpose
Deadline: 20 February (2027-02-20)
How to Apply: Submit online application through the official Türkiye Bursları Application System (TBBS).
