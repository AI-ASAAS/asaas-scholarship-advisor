Title: Russian Government State Scholarship (Education in Russia)
Official URL: https://education-in-russia.com/
Overview: Quota scholarship funded by the Russian Federation covering full higher education degree studies.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Foreign citizens from designated quota countries including Yemen.
- Successful completion of required secondary or university education.
Selection Criteria:
- Academic portfolio evaluation
- Competitive selection interview or testing
Fields of Study: Nuclear Physics, Oil & Gas Engineering, Information Technology, Medicine, Aviation
Funding: 100% tuition coverage for the entire degree duration, Russian preparatory language faculty, and subsidized student dormitory.
Required Documents: Passport with at least 18 months validity, Educational certificates with grades, Medical certificates including HIV test
Deadline: 31 January (2027-01-31)
How to Apply: Register and submit application on the Education in Russia official portal.
