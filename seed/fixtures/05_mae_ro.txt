Title: Romanian Government Scholarship (MFA Romania)
Official URL: https://www.mae.ro/en/node/10250
Overview: Annual scholarships awarded by the Romanian state through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to foreign citizens.
Eligibility Requirements:
- Non-EU foreign citizens from all countries including Yemen.
- Must not hold Romanian citizenship or asylum status in Romania.
Selection Criteria:
- Academic performance in previous studies
- Alignment with priority development fields
Fields of Study: Engineering, Economics, Agriculture, Veterinary Medicine, Political Science, Education
Funding: Free tuition, preparatory Romanian language year, free dormitory accommodation, and monthly financial support.
Required Documents: Diplomas and transcripts translated into English or French, Birth certificate copy, Passport copy, Curriculum Vitae
Deadline: 16 March (2027-03-16)
How to Apply: Submit application exclusively through the Study in Romania online platform.
